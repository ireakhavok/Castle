In the current SiegeEngine codebase, BasePanel.Render is the central rendering method for all panels. It draws scene content (via RenderInnerContent) and UIOverlay first under a full-panel scissor, then draws the PanelChrome (title bar background + close button) immediately afterward under the restored full-panel scissor. This change was made so that title bars sit above the embedded scenes and HTML content.
The current bugs are isolated to layering order only. The close button [X] drawn inside PanelChrome.Render is no longer appearing on top of its own title bar background quad — it is either hidden beneath the title bar fill or completely invisible on every panel that has HasTitleBar and IsClosable set to true. Simultaneously, the hover icons (directional arrows + center dock icon) generated by IDEDockingStrategy.Render after all panel.Render calls are now appearing underneath the title bars instead of on top of them, even though the strategy explicitly resets the scissor and viewport to full window coordinates before drawing the icons.
These two issues are the only remaining problems. The title bar itself must stay forward of scene and UI content, but the close button must sit above the title bar background and the IDEDockingStrategy hover icons must sit above every title bar during drag operations. No other visual artifacts (bottom gaps, scrolling, offsets, or aspect-ratio problems) are present in the current code.
