The IDEDockingStrategy is now in a strong state where dragging a docked panel by its title bar and releasing it away from any hover icon works perfectly: the panel cleanly becomes floating, the tree collapses, and the surviving sibling (or subtree) immediately fills the entire space with no gaps or orientation flips. Initial floating panels that spawn at startup can also be dragged correctly again.
However, the close button on any panel does not behave the same way. Instead of producing the exact same clean fill result that a successful drag/tear-out gives, closing a panel currently causes weird layout artifacts — the remaining panel gets split in the opposite direction, stretched incorrectly, or leaves blank space. The request is simple and precise: make the close action function identically to a title-bar drag/tear-out, except instead of turning the panel into a floating one, it should simply be removed entirely. The surviving sibling must instantly expand to take 100% of the space with the exact same clean, gap-free, orientation-preserving behavior we already have for drag.
Do this without touching or breaking any of the already-working drag-to-float logic, hover icons, center-square tabbing, or directional arrow splitting.
Once the close button works exactly like tear-out (clean fill on the remaining panel with no weird splits), the very next step will be to enable live splitter dragging so users can resize the ratio between split panels while keeping all current behaviors intact.
