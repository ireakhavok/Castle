1. Permanent IDE Menubar Enforcement (Phase 1 — we do this first, exactly as you requested)

The IDE basepanel (your DockedHeader panel) stays fixed at top, full width, non-draggable.
All other panels (docked or floating) are clamped so their title bar can never go above headerHeight.
Dock root always computes layout starting at Y = headerHeight.
When dragging a panel to the top edge it now snaps to “DockedTop below menubar”, never overlapping.

2. Resizing (Phase 2)

Floating panels get 5-pixel resize grips on all 4 edges + corners.
Docked panels keep the existing splitter dragging (DockSplitNode) + gain visible grip highlights.
Live size preview during resize (ghost outline).

3. Panel-to-Panel Edge Snapping + Live Preview (Phase 3)

While dragging a floating panel, we show a semi-transparent ghost rectangle + highlight the target edge.
Snap tolerance = 15 px to any docked panel’s edge (left/right/top/bottom) or screen edge (respecting header).
Drop near edge → automatically inserts a new DockSplitNode at that exact spot.
Drop in center of a tab group → converts to tab.
This solves your concern about floating panels behaving badly over other panels.

4. Extra Polish (Phase 4)

Double-click title = maximize within usable area.
Right-click title = dock/float/close menu.
All changes use your existing UIQuadRenderer for previews (no new dependencies).
