Disclaimer: The following information about Blender's rest and bind poses could be wrong or incomplete, as it is based on general knowledge and may vary by exporter version or settings.

In Blender, the rest pose is the default pose of the armature, defined by the local transforms of each bone in edit mode. When exporting to FBX with 'Z Up' and 'Y Forward', Blender applies a scene rotation to make forward +Y, which affects mesh vertices but not always bone locals consistently. Bind poses in FBX are globals at skinning time, often matching rest but stored in exporter space, leading to orientation mismatches in engines without additional flips/transposes during load. What I believe is going on is that the actual bones are able to be applied, but the armature (or maybe the root node?) is not being re-oriented, because it's not a limbnode type (armature). What is going on is that the rest pose skeleton is offset from the root node to the first actual bone in the wrong direction. and because of this, it is rendering to the right and down of the model instead of overlapping with the model correctly. What is going on with the rest vs mesh, is that the mesh vs the bind pose is rotated 180 degrees from each other, so we'll have to figure out why that is happening as well.
