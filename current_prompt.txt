The NAIP files (JP2/TIFF) are high-res color orthophotos that are geographic subsets of the terrain DEM (not 1:1 coverage, different scale/resolution). The goal is to apply them as a "skin" (filled triangles) over the wireframe mesh, but only in the correct geo location with proper scaling/alignment. Outside the texture bounds, fall back to the original cyan wireframe look. Use GeoTIFF tags (ModelTiepointTag for tiepoints, ModelPixelScaleTag for pixel size) from both the terrain heightmap and the color texture to compute per-vertex UVs in world coordinates (X=east, Y=north). Rebuild the mesh in SetColorTexture to apply the skin immediately.
